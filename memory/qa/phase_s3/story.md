## Notes (caveats first)
- Steps 1–10 were driven by `tools/story_s3.py` against the same `/api` endpoints the UI calls (not by clicking through every form); screenshots were taken with Playwright on the preview after the run (the kept run was cancelled at step 9, so booking/C360 shots show the final Cancelled state). The S2 booking wizard and payment sheet were UI-verified independently by root.
- Step 3: the Project Statement is a per-booking statement — an unsold flat has no row by design; its unsold state and price show on Project → Inventory (screenshot 03) and the inventory value on Overview.
- Step 8: overdue was shown with a backdated manual bill (no as-of shifting).
- Step 9: refund = ₹0 because the default policy deducts 10 % of agreement (₹7.2 L) capped at the ₹5 L received → forfeiture ₹5 L; the cancelled booking and archived project remain as audit history, which is why project/booking counts grow by one while tiles, customers and integrity are unchanged.

## Propagation gaps found by the story and fixed in S3
1. Payment-plan template with an `on_possession` instalment was silently skipped unless a stage was flagged `is_possession` → possession now maps to the last stage of the tower's stage plan (`routers/templates.py`).
2. Allotment letter, cost sheet and payment-schedule letter were not registered on booking; demand letters were not registered when the dues engine raised a bill → `docgen.auto_register` on booking create and on every raised instalment.
3. Cancelling a booking left its letters un-stamped and produced no cancellation letter → all generated documents are stamped VOID and a Cancellation/Refund letter is registered after the refund record exists.
4. Demand Register search by customer / booking number missed engine-raised bills (demands store ids only) → search also resolves through bookings.
5. Integrity report counted a split receipt's whole amount in one bucket → uses per-allocation parts.
