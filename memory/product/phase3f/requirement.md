# Phase 3F: Reports — 5 segments × 10 reports, one report engine, the owner's one-click Customer Status

Version: phase-3F. Features: F-48 … F-54 (see `../overview.md`). Starts only after Phase 3E (entry completion) is closed with an independent tester pass. Locked rules: `/app/memory/HANDOFF.md` §3, §5, §10 and the money rules in `../overview.md`. Gates: `../ALL_PHASES_TODO.md` "Gates". Builds on: Phase 3 report sheet (dark header, GRAND TOTAL + formula in words, legend), Reports hub tiles, `services/definitions` (one outstanding, §3R per-part ageing, waivers, discount snapshots), report cache keyed by data stamp, saved presets (`/reports/presets`), Elite controls (Combobox / DateInput / DateTimeInput), tieout, connection matrix, perf budget on demo + `kkt_volume`.

## Goal
The owner asked (2026-11-18, before sleeping): "I want 5 level segmented reports. Each segment must have 10 types of reports. 1. Customer segment (select all, many or few options, multiple type of date range and all logical filters optional) and one click report which gives me a detailed 10 important columns report — status of all customers … name, flat code, agreement value, cash component, bill raised till date, in account received, cash received, in account dues, cash dues, final status. Segment 2 Finance, 3 Booking, 4 Project inventory, 5 Leads … think of the world's best illustrated ERP and reports: simplified, segmented, beautiful reports, not randoms."

So: five segments — **Customers · Finance · Bookings · Project & inventory · Leads** — each with exactly 10 reports (50), all built on **one report engine** so every report has the same filter bar, the same 5-level drill (Project → Tower → Floor → Flat → Customer), the same sheet, totals, formula in words, exports and phone layout. Figures come only from `services/definitions`; every report total ties out. Calm Elite look; one meaningful illustration per report, never decoration.

Presentation and reporting only. No booking, payment, allocation, ledger, pricing, GST, waiver, discount, cancellation, transfer or numbering rule changes.

## Functional requirements

### 3F-A One report engine (F-48)
- REQ-259 [always] Every report shall be declared once in a server-side report registry (key, segment, title, purpose, columns with types, default columns, optional columns, filters, group levels, totals, formula in words, illustration, permission, row link) and rendered by one shared report sheet; no report has its own one-off page.
- REQ-260 [always] Every list filter shall be a multi-select with **Select all**, search, and "N selected" chips (project, tower, floor, flat type, flat status, customer, sales person, plan, payment mode, bank, charge head, lead source, lead status, visit outcome, booking status, customer status, KYC status, waiver state, etc. — whichever apply to the report); every filter is optional; "Clear all" resets.
- REQ-261 [always] Project → Tower → Floor → Flat filters shall cascade (choosing projects narrows towers, towers narrow floors and flats).
- REQ-262 [always] Every dated report shall offer date presets — Today · Yesterday · This week · Last week · This month · Last month · This quarter · Last quarter · This FY · Last FY · Custom range · As of a date — in India business days, and, where the report has more than one meaningful date, a **date basis** choice (e.g. booking date / bill date / due date / payment date / clearance date / visit date / enquiry date).
- REQ-263 [always] Reports shall offer the logical value filters that apply (e.g. amount from–to, days overdue ≥, % received from–to, only with dues / only with advance / only overdue / include cancelled), all optional.
- REQ-264 [always] Under the sheet header the report shall state its filters in one plain sentence ("21 customers · SGH, GGV · overdue 30+ days · as of 18 Nov 2026").
- REQ-265 [always] Every report shall group by any of up to 5 levels — Project → Tower → Floor → Flat → Customer (or the report's own levels, e.g. Source → Sales person → Lead) — with collapsible subtotals at each level and a grand total; tapping a row opens the next level, and the last level opens the record (customer page, booking page, bill, receipt, lead).
- REQ-266 [always] Every report shall have a default column set (≤ 10 columns) and a column chooser for optional columns, sort on any column, sticky header and first column, a totals row, and no wrapping of codes or money at 1440 px; at 390 px rows become cards.
- REQ-267 [always] Every report shall export the exact rows, columns, filters and totals on screen to Excel (.xlsx, numbers as numbers, ₹ format, totals row, filter sentence on top) and PDF (letterhead, landscape when > 7 columns, columns sized to fit — no wrapped codes or money), print, and share as a link that reopens the same view.
- REQ-268 [always] A user shall save any filtered view as a named view (per user, optionally shared with the team), reopen it from the report and from the hub, and pin up to 6 views to Home.
- REQ-269 [always] Each report shall carry at most one illustration that explains its main figure (e.g. ageing bar, collection trend, funnel, stacking plan, split bar), drawn in the Elite palette (graphite / sapphire; coral only for overdue, mint only for advance), never a decorative chart.
- REQ-270 [always] Every report total and every grand total shall come from `services/definitions` and tie out in `tools/tieout.py` to the matching source (Outstanding, Collections, Bills vs Collection, Cash vs Account, inventory counts, lead funnel …).
- REQ-271 [always] Every report shall open within the perf budget on `kkt_volume` (1,500 bookings) — p95 ≤ 300 ms first page, ≤ 100 ms cached — with server-side filtering, sorting, grouping and paging, batched queries and the data-stamp cache.
- REQ-272 [always] Report access shall follow the permission matrix per report; Sales sees only sales-safe reports and the display (masked) inventory; money columns hidden from roles without money rights.
- REQ-273 [unwanted] If a report cannot load, then the sheet shall show "Couldn't load — Retry" with the filters kept; if the filters match nothing, it shall say so and offer "Clear filters".

### 3F-B Reports hub (F-49)
- REQ-274 [always] The Reports hub shall show the 5 segments in order Customers · Finance · Bookings · Project & inventory · Leads, each with its 10 report tiles (live headline figure + scope caption, as today), a segment summary line, and the user's saved views; Activity log and Data quality move to a small "Control room" row at the foot (not a segment).
- REQ-275 [always] Old report links shall keep working (redirect to the new key); the Exports page keeps every scheduled export.

### 3F-C Customers segment (F-50)
- REQ-276 [event] When the user taps **"Customer status · one click"** (top of the hub and top of the Customers segment), the system shall open, with no filter needed, every live customer booking (one row per booking, customer name repeated; group by customer gives subtotals) with exactly these 10 columns in this order: **Customer name · Flat code (project · tower · flat) · Agreement value · Cash component · Bills raised till date · Account received · Cash received · Account dues · Cash dues · Final status**, a totals row, and Excel / PDF in one tap.
- REQ-277 [always] In Customer status: Agreement value = Cash component + Account component (current agreement after revisions); Bills raised till date = instalment bills (Cash + Account) raised up to the as-of date; Account / Cash received = money settled into that share as of the date (cleared, per the ledger); Account / Cash dues = that share's bills raised − received − approved waivers (never below 0; advance shown in status); Final status = Overdue N days · Due on <date> · All clear · Advance ₹X · Cancelled · Transferred, from the one definition. Optional columns: Account component, Other charges due, Overdue ₹, Next due date, Phone, Sales person, Plan, Stage k of n. Formula in words: "Bills raised ₹A − Account received ₹B − Cash received ₹C − waived ₹W = dues ₹D (Account ₹E + Cash ₹F)". (interpreted)
- REQ-278 [always] Customer status shall offer the As-of date (default today) so the owner can see every customer's status on any past date, plus all REQ-260…263 filters (project/tower/floor/flat, final status multi, sales person, plan, booking date range, dues from–to, days overdue ≥, include cancelled).
- REQ-279 [always] The Customers segment shall hold exactly these 10 reports: 1 Customer status (one click) · 2 Customer statement (one customer, all bookings: bills, receipts, waivers, running balance, printable on letterhead) · 3 Overdue & ageing by customer (bands, days overdue, last payment, sales person) · 4 Advance holders · 5 Collection call list (due and overdue in the chosen window with phone, last follow-up, next follow-up) · 6 KYC, agreement & documents pending · 7 Payment behaviour (on-time %, average days late, bounced cheques, waivers per customer) · 8 Customer directory (contacts, co-applicants, city, source, sales person, active / inactive / merged) · 9 Service requests & SLA · 10 Cancelled & transferred customers (refunds paid / pending, forfeits, new flat). (interpreted)

### 3F-D Finance segment (F-51)
- REQ-280 [always] The Finance segment shall hold exactly: 1 Collections (Cash / Account / Other by period, mode, bank; carried-from-before + received bridge) · 2 Day book — Roznamcha (opening + in − out = closing, by day / range) · 3 Bank-wise receipts · 4 Cheque register (in hand / cleared / bounced, ageing of cheques in hand) · 5 Bills vs Collection (billed − received − waived, recovery %) · 6 Outstanding & ageing (5-level drill) · 7 Cash vs Account split · 8 Other charges & GST (by head: demanded, received, waived, due; GST billed, credit notes, net GST) · 9 Receivables forecast (next 30 / 60 / 90 days and by expected stage) · 10 Waivers & credit notes (states, GST credit, Section 34 window). Existing reports move onto the engine with their totals unchanged (tieout before = after). (interpreted)

### 3F-E Bookings segment (F-52)
- REQ-281 [always] The Bookings segment shall hold exactly: 1 Booking register (date, number, customer, flat, plan, agreement, Cash / Account, sales person, status) · 2 Sales summary (bookings, value, area, average rate by period × project / tower / flat type / sales person) · 3 Discount register · 4 Plan & stage position (plan, stage k of n, % billed, % received per booking) · 5 Agreements pending / signed (days since booking) · 6 Cancellations & refunds · 7 Transfers, revisions & plan changes · 8 Sales team performance · 9 Realised rate (agreed vs list per sq ft by tower / flat type) · 10 Booking health (booking age, % collected vs % billed, overdue). (interpreted)

### 3F-F Project & inventory segment (F-53)
- REQ-282 [always] The Project & inventory segment shall hold exactly: 1 Project statement · 2 Inventory status (Available / Hold / Booked / Sold by project / tower / floor / type) · 3 Stacking plan (floors × flats grid coloured by status, tap a flat to open it — the segment's illustration) · 4 Price list & unsold stock value · 5 Hold register · 6 Landowner & masked units · 7 Parking inventory (slots by type: available / allotted) · 8 Construction vs plan · 9 Stage bills by tower (stage done, bills raised, received) · 10 Sales velocity (units per month per tower, months of stock left). Sales role sees the display (masked) status. (interpreted)

### 3F-G Leads segment (F-54)
- REQ-283 [always] The Leads segment shall hold exactly: 1 Lead funnel (enquiry → visit → booking) · 2 Lead register · 3 Source performance (leads, visits, bookings, conversion %, by source) · 4 Follow-ups due & missed · 5 Site visit register (scheduled / done / no-show) · 6 Site visit conversion (by outcome, project, sales person) · 7 Lost leads & reasons · 8 Response time (first response distribution) · 9 Team pipeline (open leads, follow-ups, visits, bookings per sales person) · 10 Lead ageing (days since enquiry / since last action, stale leads). (interpreted)

### 3F-H Proof
- REQ-284 [always] Each of the 50 reports shall have: a tieout line, a phone-sweep entry (Night + Day), a perf-budget entry (demo + volume), an export check (xlsx + pdf open and totals equal screen), and a screenshot (Night + Day, 1440 + 390); the connection matrix gains a "reports" surface per segment so every money action shows up in the right reports.
